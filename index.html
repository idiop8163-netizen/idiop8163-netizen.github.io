Perfume 101: Learn the Basics

Learn the essential perfume terminology, how to choose the right scent, and how to wear fragrance like a pro.

Notes Explained

Top notes are the first impression, middle notes form the heart, and base notes give the fragrance its lasting depth.

Fragrance Types

Eau de Toilette is lighter, while Eau de Parfum is stronger and lasts longer. Perfume extract is the most concentrated option.

How to Apply

Spray on pulse points like wrists and neck. Avoid rubbing to preserve the fragrance structure.

Tips for Choosing

Think about season, occasion, and personal style. Try scents on skin and allow them to develop before deciding.
