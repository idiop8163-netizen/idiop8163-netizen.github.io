Perfume 101: Learn the Basics

Learn the essential perfume terminology, how to choose the right scent, and how to wear fragrance like a pro.

Notes Explained

The top notes are the first impression, the heart notes carry the character, and the base notes are the long-lasting foundation.

Fragrance Types

Eaux de Parfum deliver stronger, longer-lasting scent than Eau de Toilette, with a richer concentration of signature ingredients.

How to Apply

Spray lightly on pulse points like wrist and neck. Let the fragrance develop naturally instead of rubbing for best projection.

Why Sauvage Works

Ambroxan and bergamot create a fresh yet bold profile, while cedarwood and tonka bean give it durable depth.

Fragrance glossary

Top Notes
The first impression of a fragrance, usually bright and refreshing.
Heart Notes
The core character of a scent that appears after the top notes fade.
Base Notes
The long-lasting foundation that supports the fragrance and holds it together.
Sillage
How far the scent projects from the wearer after application.
Longevity
How long the fragrance remains noticeable on skin.
